Sticky geometry and sticky section titles.

Text one.

This first block scrolls up while the circle and section title stay fixed in the background.

Text two.

The section label now belongs to the sticky layer. That's why it doesn't scroll away with the text.

Text three.

When this section ends, the circle and sticky title release and the next composition appears.

02 / SQUARE

Text one.

The square stays centered and the section identifier stays fixed in an editorial position.

Text two.

The texts scroll normally because they live in another layer, outside the sticky container.

Text three.

This pattern allows building sections with a persistent background and moving reading flow.

03 / LINES

Text one.

The lines work as a fixed structure behind the content.

Text two.

Even though the geometry is still, scrolling the text creates a sense of movement between layers.

Text three.

The 03 / LINES title stays visible during the whole section.

04 / GRID

Text one.

Text two.

The grid and the section title stay fixed while the text crosses the composition.

Text three.
