SCROLL EFFECT

ZOOM TO SCROLL

Background images expand or shrink as you leave each section.

01 / ZOOM IN

The image expands as you leave.

As you scroll past this section, the background scales up. Scroll works like a camera moving closer.

02 / ZOOM OUT

The image shrinks as you leave.

This image starts zoomed in. As you leave the section, it slowly reduces and reveals more context.

03 / DRAMATIC ZOOM

The scene becomes texture.

The zoom is stronger. The image stops being landscape and turns into an abstract surface.

04 / REVEAL OUT

The camera pulls back.

The background starts very close and moves away as you scroll. The section leaves revealing openness.
