FINAL SCROLL STUDY

SCROLLYTELLING

A story told with scrolling. Each scroll activates a scene, changes an image, and reveals a new part of the story.

01 04

01 / SETUP

First, the world appears.

In scrollytelling, scroll works like a timeline. You don't just jump from section to section: you move through a sequence.

02 / CHANGE

Then, the scene responds.

When this block enters the active zone, JavaScript detects the change and activates a new image in the background.

03 / FOCUS

The text guides the eye.

The image stays sticky, but the text moves on. This separation creates a clear relationship between narrative and visual atmosphere.

04 / RELEASE

Finally, the story releases.

When the last step ends, the user leaves the sticky scene and returns to the normal page flow.
